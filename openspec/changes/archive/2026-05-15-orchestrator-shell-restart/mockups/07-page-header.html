Notities
P · Simple

Title + sub + actions

  • Title left, primary action right — predictable
  • No tabs, no crumbs — content does the talking
  • Nested pages would add tabs / crumbs as needed
  • Cheapest, most flexible for future pages
Q · Eyebrow + tabs

Contextual

  • Eyebrow pill (date / status / context tag)
  • Tabs slot under title for in-page sections
  • Reads more "designed" out of the gate
  • Risk: feels heavy on pages without tabs
R · Breadcrumb + tabs

Deep-nav ready

  • Top crumb for any nested route (e.g. Patiënten ▸ De Smet)
  • Title + meta-sub (status, step, since)
  • Tabs always under title — sub-page navigation
  • Best for detail pages, overkill for landing pages
  • Variant rule: crumbs only when depth > 1
Note

Picking one means the PageHeader component carries every slot by default — eyebrow, crumb, title, sub, actions, tabs — and consumers fill what they need. P keeps the slot list short; R defines all slots up front.
