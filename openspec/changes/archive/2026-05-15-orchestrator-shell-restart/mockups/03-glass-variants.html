F · Safe

Subtle

  • Soft neutral gradient page bg, no color cast
  • White-card active state with hairline shadow
  • Reads as "modern light Notion-ish"
  • Cheapest to ship, fewest edge cases
G · Bold

Tinted backdrop

  • Page bg has teal-tinted radial gradients (top-left + bottom-right)
  • Active item: full teal gradient pill, white text
  • Sidebar reads as a floating panel hovering over content
  • Risk: gradients can fight with content; needs measurement
H · Aurora

Very new

  • Aurora mesh background (multi-radial teal blooms + dot grid)
  • Strong glass: 24px blur, saturated, inset highlight
  • Active item = white-glass pill; icons in tiny rounded glass tiles
  • "Online" pill, pill-shaped date tag, gradient brand mark
  • Risk: most expensive look to keep consistent across pages
