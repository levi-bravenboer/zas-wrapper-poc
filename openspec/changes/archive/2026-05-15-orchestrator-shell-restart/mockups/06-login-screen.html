M · Centered card

Card on aurora

  • Glass card mirrors sidebar treatment (consistent vocabulary)
  • 380 px focused form, easy on the eyes
  • Brand mark + title + form stacked vertical
  • Bg gradient does the visual work
  • Cheapest, most flexible
N · Split

Brand + form

  • Left teal panel for brand + tagline, right for form
  • More "marketing" — sets product personality first impression
  • Glass shell wraps both halves
  • Slot for hospital crest / co-brand later
  • Costs more layout work, less optimal on mobile
O · Minimal

No card

  • Form sits directly on bg, no card chrome
  • Most restrained — focus on form alone
  • Brand mark + title floating above inputs
  • Risk: feels under-designed for first impression
  • Strong on mobile (no panel to fight with viewport)
