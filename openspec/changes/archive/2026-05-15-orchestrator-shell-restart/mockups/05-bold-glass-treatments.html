I · Flush square

Anchored

  • No corner radius — reads as part of window chrome
  • Max vertical real estate for nav (no 32 px gutters)
  • Cleanest feel under HiX embed
  • Glass effect still visible but contained
  • Most space-efficient
J · Flush rounded-R

Compromise

  • Flush top/left/bottom — sidebar feels anchored
  • 18 px rounded right edge — softness without floating
  • Reads as "panel embedded in app shell"
  • Decent under embed + good as standalone
K · Floating light

Refined floater

  • Same 18 px floating panel from earlier
  • Active state: white-glass pill, teal icon (not full teal fill)
  • Less visual weight per item, plays nice with badges
  • Best when you want the sidebar to feel like a separate surface
L · Flush + grain

Tactile

  • Flush, higher base opacity (70%)
  • Subtle teal-dot grain inside sidebar
  • Inset shadow on right edge for depth
  • Most "material", least "purely glass"
